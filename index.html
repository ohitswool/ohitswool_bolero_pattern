A free made-to-measure pattern

Hi, friends!

I’ve released a simple free pattern before, but this is my first full, detailed pattern. I’m not a professional designer—just a fellow knitter who started knitting seriously this winter, about 10 months ago. I’m still learning, so I wouldn’t recommend this as your very first knitting pattern.

There may be mistakes, and I’ll continue updating the pattern as I receive feedback or spot them myself. Before you cast on, please come back and make sure you have the latest version. I’ll announce any major updates or corrections on Instagram @ohitswool, which is my only social account. I hope you’ll still give it a try and enjoy making it with me!

Needles

Main: 4 mm (US 6) for my samples, or the size needed to obtain your gauge.

Cables: 40 cm (16 in) and 60 or 80 cm (24 or 32 in) circular needles.

Edging: circular needle 0.5 mm and 1 mm smaller; 3.5 mm (US 4) and 3 mm (US 2.5) for my samples.

Notions

20 mm (¾ in) buttons, stitch markers, darning needle, scissors, and scrap yarn or spare needles for holding live stitches.

Pattern at a glance

The back and fronts are worked flat and joined. The sleeves are worked separately, with short rows, pleated, and sewn into the armholes.

Credits & inspiration: I learned buttonhole and short-row techniques from @loupystudio, the body construction from @femi.knit, and the puff-sleeve construction from @madeby.rosh. I learned I-cord edging and box-pleat techniques from various YouTube tutorials, including those linked throughout the pattern. I can’t remember every resource I learned from, but I’m grateful to everyone who shares their knitting knowledge online.
